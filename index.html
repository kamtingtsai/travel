文明时间感A sense of historical time

Lycia / Caria先有安纳托利亚本土传统，之后才不断与希腊世界互相渗透。Anatolian traditions existed first, then increasingly interwove with the Greek world.
Archaic & Classical爱奥尼亚城市、神谕、港口和自然哲学塑造了新的城市文化。Ionian cities, oracles, harbors and natural philosophy shaped a new urban culture.
Hellenistic亚历山大之后，希腊语与希腊式城市文化进一步扩散，但地方性从未消失。After Alexander, Greek language and urban forms spread further without erasing local identities.
Roman剧场、浴场、港口、道路和公共建筑把这些城市推入更大的帝国网络。Theaters, baths, ports, roads and public buildings drew these cities into a larger imperial network.
